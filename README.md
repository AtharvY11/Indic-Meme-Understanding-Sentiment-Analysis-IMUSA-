## 📌 Overview

**IMUSA (Indic Meme Understanding & Sentiment Analysis)** is an AI-focused project dedicated to understanding and analysing memes in the context of Indic languages.

Memes are a popular form of digital communication that combine images, embedded text, cultural references, humour, and emotions to communicate ideas and opinions.

Understanding memes is challenging because their actual meaning often depends on the relationship between textual and visual information.

This project explores the application of Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing techniques to understand meme content and analyse the sentiments expressed through it.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Explore AI-based approaches for understanding meme content.
- Analyse sentiment expressed through meme-related information.
- Investigate the challenges associated with Indic language memes.
- Understand the relationship between textual and visual information.
- Apply machine learning and NLP techniques to meme analysis.
- Explore automated approaches for interpreting digital meme content.

## ❗ Problem Statement

Memes frequently communicate emotions, opinions, humour, sarcasm, and social messages through a combination of text and images.

Traditional text-based sentiment analysis may not adequately capture the intended meaning of a meme because important contextual information is often conveyed through its visual content.

Furthermore, Indic language memes introduce additional challenges involving regional languages, linguistic variations, and cultural context.

IMUSA explores these challenges through AI-based meme understanding and sentiment analysis.

## 💡 Proposed Approach

The project investigates computational approaches for analysing meme content and understanding the sentiment associated with it.

The overall research direction focuses on:

1. **Data Collection:** Working with the available meme datasets.
2. **Data Preparation:** Preparing the data for analysis and experimentation.
3. **Content Understanding:** Investigating textual and visual information.
4. **Sentiment Analysis:** Analysing the sentiment expressed through meme content.
5. **Output Generation:** Organising the generated analysis and experimental results.
